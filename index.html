The Program

From zero to released
in 6 weeks.

Weekly 1-on-1 lessons. Built around you, streamlined for creating your first single.

Want Ableton and electronic production without formal piano training? Try Electronic Music Lab.

Week 1

Discovery & First Sounds

We map your taste, your goals, your sound. You'll touch the piano from day one and learn the building blocks of how top-charting tracks are made.

Week 2

Piano Foundations

The essential chords, rhythms, and melodies behind every great song. You'll build the muscle memory and ear to start hearing music like a producer.

Week 3

From Idea To Loop

We work with a melody or chord progression you've come up with and build it into a loop with mood, groove, and direction.

Week 4

Into the DAW

Step into Ableton Live and learn the fundamentals of music production. You'll record, layer, and shape your idea into something that actually sounds like a track.

Week 5

Arrangement

Turn your loop into a full song with intro, verse, chorus, and outro. Learn how to keep a listener hooked from the first second to the last.

Week 6

Mix, Master, Release

Final mix, master, and cover art. Then we release your song to Spotify, Apple Music, and every major platform. You finish the program as a published artist.

Result: You're a released artist.

Then we go again, and your next song will be even better.

Student Releases

Every release in this section started the same way: a student with no experience and an idea. What you're hearing is what happens when the full program comes together: piano, production, and a finished song on streaming platforms.

HARPER LEAVE

Harper is a pop artist who came in wanting to take her music somewhere new. She had the songs, but wanted to move past acoustic and into something more electronic. She worked on her mixing, her production, and finding her sound. Leave is what came out of that.

L'MARCO BRING IT BACK - PT. 1

ZERE LET ME SHOW IT

Zere is an R&B singer who wanted to learn how to make her own beats and stop waiting on other producers. She brought the voice, the vibe, and the vision. Let Me Show It is the result. She also discovered that the studio makes pretty good content, so she kept coming back for both.

GAIUS TERRAN ROAD TO NOWHERE

Gaius came in with several songs already in progress and a clear creative vision. The goal was to finish them without losing what made them his. Road to Nowhere is one of those tracks, taken further than he imagined while staying exactly true to where he wanted to go.

ELENA SANTIAGO WHERE THE PLAIN ENDS

Elena is an intermediate pianist with a passion for film and cinematic music. She already had the feel, she just needed the tools to turn it into a finished track. Where the Plain Ends is her first original release and the start of the portfolio she is building.

ANDY B Fruit Punch

Andy B came to First Note at 10 with a love for Hip Hop and zero production experience. He started on piano, white keys only, found his sound, then moved to drums and 808s. Fruit Punch is proof that the fundamentals are all you need.

MICAH CHANG PINK SUMMER

Micah came to First Note at 11 with a love for K-pop and a head full of melodies. She started with the hook, then built every instrument around it. Pink Summer is her first production and she's already looking for a vocalist to jump on it.
