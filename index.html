The Program

From zero to released
in 12 weeks.

Weekly 1-on-1 lessons. Built around you. Just the path to your first single.

Weeks 1–2

Discovery & First Sounds

We map your taste, your goals, your sound. You'll touch the piano from day one and learn the building blocks of how songs work.

Weeks 3–4

Piano Foundations

The essential chords, rhythms, and melodies behind every great song. You'll build the muscle memory and ear to start hearing music like a producer.

Weeks 5–6

From Idea To Loop

We work with a melody or chord progression you've come up with and build it into a loop with mood, groove, and direction.

Weeks 7–8

Into the DAW

Step into Ableton Live and learn the fundamentals of music production. You'll record, layer, and shape your idea into something that actually sounds like a track.

Weeks 9–10

Arrangement

Turn your loop into a full song with intro, verse, chorus, and outro. Learn how to keep a listener hooked from the first second to the last.

Weeks 11–12

Mix, Master, Release

Final mix, master, and cover art — then we release your song to Spotify, Apple Music, and every major platform. You finish the program as a published artist.

Result: You're a released artist.

Then we go again — and your next song will be even better.
